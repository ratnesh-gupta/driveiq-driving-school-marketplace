## Vision

# 2. Problem Statement

## Current Situation

Most driving schools already use Google Business Profile.

However, they struggle with:

* Incomplete profiles
* Low review response rates
* Poor profile optimization
* Irregular photo updates
* Lack of Google Posts
* No performance tracking
* No lead attribution
* No local SEO expertise

Most schools manage Google Business manually.
