# Driving School Operating System (DSOS)

# Product Domain Architecture

Version 1.0

---

# Product Vision

Build a unified platform that helps:

* Learners find and enroll in driving schools
* Driving schools manage operations
* Trainers manage learners and schedules
* School owners grow their business
* Government guidance and compliance information remain accessible

The platform consists of multiple business domains.

---

# Domain 1: Marketplace Domain

Purpose:
Acquire and convert prospective learners.

Modules:

* School Discovery
* Search
* Filters
* Comparison Engine
* Reviews & Ratings
* Local SEO Pages
* Knowledge Center
* Government Resources

Primary Users:

* Visitors
* Prospective Learners

Key Outputs:

* Leads
* School Visibility
* SEO Traffic

---

# Domain 2: Lead Management Domain

Purpose:
Manage inquiries before enrollment.

Modules:

* Lead Capture
* Lead Assignment
* Lead Pipeline
* Follow-Up Tracking
* Inquiry Notes
* Conversion Tracking

Lead Lifecycle:

New Lead
→ Contacted
→ Follow-Up
→ Interested
→ Enrolled
→ Converted To Learner

Primary Users:

* School Owner
* School Manager

---

* School Owner

* Learner

Lifecycle:
